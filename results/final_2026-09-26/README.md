# Final audit outputs (recorded 26 September 2026)

These files are the recorded judgments and computed tabulations reported in the accompanying manuscript, "US medical device surveillance cannot retrieve adverse events across products sharing a foundation model: an audit of identifier schemas and record linkage" (T. Aoki; under peer review). The codebook and blank templates that fixed the rules before any confirmatory judgment (2 September 2026) and the three addenda (3 September 2026) are in `codebook/`. Changes made after judgments were recorded are listed in `CHANGELOG_2026-09-26.md` at the repository root.

Codes. C1 (query key): none; mention (free text only); structured capture (a structured record that cannot be joined from event records); cross-product retrieval (C1 met). These four grades relabel the referent and reach grades of `codebook/codebook_v1.md`: none = R0; mention = R1; structured capture = R2 with Q0 or Q1; cross-product retrieval = R2 with Q2. The codebook states C1 as met at R2 with Q≥1 and requires Q2 for horizontal aggregation of event records; the manuscript reports the Q2 condition as C1 met. No audited data source or mechanism is graded R2 with Q1, so no result depends on the difference. The proportion of records carrying a valid key, called reach in the manuscript, is the coverage of addendum v1.2. C2 (signal definition) and C3 (scheduled review) combine the codebook's paired conditions C2a/C2b and C3a/C3b: Present (both met); Absent (a specific signal definition or review procedure is documented but is not keyed to a shared upstream component, or does not concern adverse events); Not documented. "(flag)" marks a document that could be cited against an absence judgment. The rules for boundary cases, including how the data-source-level C2 and C3 codes follow from the document-level codes, are stated in `codebook/codebook_v1.4_addendum_classification_by_rule.md`. For mechanisms (Table 2), C2 and C3 are coded Met or Not met as described.

Computed outputs underlying Supplementary Table 3 (master-file scan, DEVICE-file join, AccessGUDID results of 2 September 2026, heuristic token search, including the 212 report–field units deposited unclassified) are in `out/`; hashes of the downloaded FDA files are in `data/MANIFEST.md`. Figures are regenerated by `scripts/figures_manuscript.py`.

CSV files are UTF-8 with a byte-order mark so that they open correctly in Excel.
